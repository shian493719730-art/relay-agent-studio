# Community conduct

Be respectful, concrete and helpful. Critique ideas and code, not people. People with different backgrounds, abilities and experience are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not post credentials or personal task data. Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to keep collaboration safe and productive. For a concern involving private information, request a private maintainer channel without publishing that information. Enforcement is contextual; participation is not a guarantee that every proposal will be accepted.
